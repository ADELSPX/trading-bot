# Gamma Strategy: Abu Fahad Protocol

## Color Coding (Timeframes)
- 🟣 **Purple:** Monthly (Strongest)
- 🟡 **Yellow:** Weekly
- 🔵 **Blue:** Daily
- 🟢/🔴 **Green/Red:** Hourly (1H)

## Gamma Tower Hierarchy (Power levels)
1. 🔴 **Red:** Sovereign Liquidity (Strongest)
2. 🟡 **Yellow:** High Power
3. 🔵 **Blue:** Medium Power
4. ⚪ **White:** Intraday/Momentum (Weakest)

## The "Bus Station" (🚎) Rule
- **Call Entry (🟢):** 5-minute candle closes ABOVE the tower WITHOUT touching it.
- **Put Entry (🔴):** 5-minute candle closes BELOW the tower WITHOUT touching it.
- **Stop Loss:** Strict. Exit if a candle of the opposite color closes on the other side of the entry tower.

## Reda Al-Walidayn (Parental Blessing) Strategy
- **Type:** CALL only.
- **Timing:** Weekly contracts (Friday expiry).
- **Execution:** Buy at the lowest "Far" Gamma towers (Bottom of range) when price is oversold.
- **Scale:** Buy 1/3 position at the first level, 2/3 at the deeper level.
